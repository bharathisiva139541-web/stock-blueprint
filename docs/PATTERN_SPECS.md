## Rules of the implemented patterns

### Shared definitions (every rule below uses them)

- ATR = Wilder ATR(14) of the candle BEFORE the pattern's first candle (its size yardstick, known before the pattern starts).
- Long body = body >= 0.7 x ATR; small body = body <= 0.3 x ATR; doji = body <= 10 % of its own range (range > 0). Upper shadow = high - max(open, close), lower shadow = min(open, close) - low; "% of the range" = of that candle's high - low.
- c1, c2 ... = the pattern's candles, oldest first; the signal = the close of its last candle. Entry = that close or the next open (the Lab's "Buy"); stop reference = the lowest low of the pattern's candles and trigger = their highest high, unless a rule says otherwise.
- Fresh = the close above the level while the close before was not above ITS level (one signal per breakout, not every day the price stays above).
- Relative volume = the candle's volume / the average volume of the 20 candles BEFORE it.
- Trend context (Lab "Before the pattern", reversal patterns only): "After a fall" keeps a pattern only when the candle before it closed under its 20-SMA and that SMA fell over the 5 candles before. Continuation patterns (K16, K26, K27) always need the uptrend: the candle before closed above its 20-SMA, which rose over the 5 candles before.
- Invalidated by a close under the stop reference before the entry. Lengths are candles of the chosen timeframe unless a rule gives one per timeframe. Every value uses only the candle and earlier ones (checked: cut / changed-future tests).

### K01 Hammer (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. One candle, range >= 0.5 x ATR, either colour.
2. Body > 10 % and <= 35 % of the range (a doji is K03).
3. Lower shadow >= 2 x the body and >= 55 % of the range; upper shadow <= 10 % of the range.

_In words:_ A hammer: a small body at the top and a long lower shadow - sellers pushed the price down during the candle and buyers pushed it back up.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K02 Inverted Hammer (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. One candle, range >= 0.5 x ATR, either colour.
2. Body > 10 % and <= 35 % of the range.
3. Upper shadow >= 2 x the body and >= 55 % of the range; lower shadow <= 10 % of the range.

_In words:_ An inverted hammer: a small body at the bottom and a long upper shadow - buyers tried to lift the price after a fall.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K03 Dragonfly Doji (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. One candle, range >= 0.5 x ATR.
2. A doji (body <= 10 % of the range) with an upper shadow <= 10 % and a lower shadow >= 70 % of the range.

_In words:_ A dragonfly doji: it opened and closed at the top after a long dip during the candle - the dip was fully bought.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K04 Bullish Marubozu (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. Green, long body.
2. Upper and lower shadows each <= 5 % of the range.

_In words:_ A bullish marubozu: a long green candle with almost no shadows - buyers were in control from the open to the close.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K05 Bullish Belt Hold (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. Green, long body, body >= 60 % of the range.
2. Opens at its low: lower shadow <= 3 % of the range.
3. Opens below the close of the candle before.

_In words:_ A bullish belt hold: it opened lower, at its low, and then rose all the way - a strong turn after a fall.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K06 Long Lower Shadow (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. Range >= 1 x ATR (a big candle).
2. Lower shadow >= 60 % of the range.
3. Close in the upper half of the range (close location value >= 0).

_In words:_ A long lower shadow: a big candle whose low was strongly rejected - it closed in its upper half.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K07 Bullish Closing Marubozu (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. Green, long body, body >= 60 % of the range.
2. Closes at its high: upper shadow <= 2 % of the range; a lower shadow > 5 % (otherwise it is K04).

_In words:_ A closing marubozu: a long green candle that closed at its high - buyers held on until the close.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K08 Bullish Engulfing (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: `engBody` 0

1. c1 = the candle before, c2 = the signal candle (OHLC only).
2. c1 red (close < open), c2 green (close > open).
3. c2's body engulfs c1's: c2 open <= c1 close and c2 close >= c1 open, and c2's body is bigger than c1's (= the sheet's column W rule, AB_ENGULFING_REQUIRE_BIGGER).
4. engBody > 0: c2 body at least engBody x ATR(14) (Wilder) of the candle before c2 - leaves out tiny candles.
5. Confirmed at c2's close: signal candle = c2; first entry = c2's close (or the next open).
6. Stop reference = the lower of c1's and c2's lows; trigger = c2's high.
7. Invalidated once a later close is under that stop reference (the entry is not taken after that).

_In words:_ A red candle was followed by a bigger green candle whose body covered all of the red body - buyers took over the whole of the day before. The stop goes under the lower of the two lows.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K09 Piercing Line (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. c1 red; c2 opens under c1's LOW.
2. c2 closes above the middle of c1's body and below c1's open.
3. No size rule (the sheet's rule).

_In words:_ A piercing line: after a red candle the next one opened below its low and closed above the middle of its body.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K10 Bullish Harami (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. c1 red, c2 green.
2. c2's body inside c1's body (c2 open >= c1 close, c2 close <= c1 open), smaller than c1's.
3. c2's low above c1's low (the sheet's rule).

_In words:_ A bullish harami: a small green candle inside the body of the red one before - the fall stopped.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K11 Bullish Harami Cross (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. c1 red, long body.
2. c2 a doji with its whole body inside c1's body (between c1's close and open).

_In words:_ A harami cross: a doji inside the body of a long red candle - the selling stopped.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K12 Tweezer Bottom (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. c1 red, c2 green.
2. Lows equal within 1.25 % of their average.
3. Bodies differ by <= 25 % of the bigger, lower shadows by <= 30 %, both lower shadows > 0 (the sheet's rule).

_In words:_ A tweezer bottom: two candles with the same low, a red then a green one - that low was defended twice.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K13 Matching Low (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: 

1. c1 red with a long body, c2 red.
2. Their closes equal within 0.1 % of c1's close.

_In words:_ A matching low: two red candles closing at the same price - the fall found a floor.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.

### K14 Bullish Counterattack (version 1)

### I01 RSI Bullish Divergence (version 1)

7. Stop reference = low(p2); trigger = the highest high from p2 to the signal candle.
8. Invalidated by a close under low(p2) before the entry.

_In words:_ Price made a lower swing low but RSI made a higher low - the fall was losing strength (a bullish divergence). The signal came only when the second low was confirmed by the candles after it, never on the low itself.
