## Rules of the implemented patterns

### K08 Bullish Engulfing (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: `engBody` 0

1. c1 = the candle before, c2 = the signal candle (OHLC only).
2. c1 red (close < open), c2 green (close > open).
3. c2's body engulfs c1's: c2 open <= c1 close and c2 close >= c1 open, and c2's body is bigger than c1's (= the sheet's column W rule, AB_ENGULFING_REQUIRE_BIGGER).
4. engBody > 0: c2 body at least engBody x ATR(14) (Wilder) of the candle before c2 - leaves out tiny candles.
5. Confirmed at c2's close: signal candle = c2; first entry = c2's close (or the next open).
6. Stop reference = the lower of c1's and c2's lows; trigger = c2's high.
7. Invalidated once a later close is under that stop reference (the entry is not taken after that).

### B01 20-Day High Breakout (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: `boBuf` 0, `boVol` 0

1. OHLC + volume.
2. Level = the highest HIGH of 20 candles before the signal candle (D: 20 days; W: 20 weeks; 4H: 20 candles) - the signal candle itself is not in it.
3. Signal: the close is above level x (1 + boBuf %%) AND the previous close was not above ITS level (a fresh breakout - a stock staying above it does not signal again every day).
4. boVol > 0: the signal candle's volume at least boVol x the average of the 20 candles before it.
5. Confirmed at the signal candle's close: first entry = its close (or the next open).
6. Stop reference = the signal candle's low; trigger = the level.
7. Invalidated once a later close is back under the level before the entry.

### I01 RSI Bullish Divergence (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: `rsiN` 14, `divPivot` 5, `divMaxGap` 60, `divRsiMax` 100

1. Close prices for RSI (Wilder, rsiN = 14), lows for the swing lows.
2. Swing lows = structure.pivots with divPivot candles each side (a low lower than the divPivot lows before it and not above the divPivot lows after it). A swing low at candle p is KNOWN only at candle p + divPivot.
3. Two consecutive swing lows p1 < p2, at least divPivot and at most divMaxGap candles apart (60).
4. Price makes a lower low: low(p2) < low(p1). RSI makes a higher low: RSI(p2) > RSI(p1).
5. divRsiMax < 100: RSI(p1) must be at most divRsiMax (e.g. 40 = the first low was oversold-ish).
6. Confirmed on candle p2 + divPivot (the 2nd swing low becomes known): signal candle = that candle - never p2.
7. Stop reference = low(p2); trigger = the highest high from p2 to the signal candle.
8. Invalidated by a close under low(p2) before the entry.
