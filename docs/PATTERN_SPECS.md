## Rules of the implemented patterns

### Shared definitions (every rule below uses them)

- ATR = Wilder ATR(14) of the candle BEFORE the pattern's first candle (its size yardstick, known before the pattern starts).
- Long body = body >= 0.7 x ATR; small body = body <= 0.3 x ATR; doji = body <= 10 % of its own range (range > 0). Upper shadow = high - max(open, close), lower shadow = min(open, close) - low; "% of the range" = of that candle's high - low.
- c1, c2 ... = the pattern's candles, oldest first; the signal = the close of its last candle. Entry = that close or the next open (the Lab's "Buy"); stop reference = the lowest low of the pattern's candles and trigger = their highest high, unless a rule says otherwise.
- Fresh = the close above the level while the close before was not above ITS level (one signal per breakout, not every day the price stays above).
- Relative volume = the candle's volume / the average volume of the 20 candles BEFORE it.
- Trend context (Lab "Before the pattern", reversal patterns only): "After a fall" keeps a pattern only when the candle before it closed under its 20-SMA and that SMA fell over the 5 candles before. Continuation patterns (K16, K26, K27) always need the uptrend: the candle before closed above its 20-SMA, which rose over the 5 candles before.
- Invalidated by a close under the stop reference before the entry. Lengths are candles of the chosen timeframe unless a rule gives one per timeframe. Every value uses only the candle and earlier ones (checked: cut / changed-future tests).
- Swings (phase 3): a swing low = a low lower than the patSwing (5) candles before it and not above the patSwing candles after it (a swing high mirrored - structure.pivots, the same as the Lab's market structure). A swing is KNOWN patSwing candles after it; "the latest swing low" at a candle = the latest one known before that candle. A pattern whose signal is the swing itself signals on the candle it becomes known, never on the swing candle.
- Zones (SMC): a bullish FVG = the 3rd candle's low above the 1st candle's high, the gap at least 0.1 x ATR (zone = that gap); a bearish FVG mirrored. A zone's first touch = the first later candle whose low reaches the zone (its top, or its middle where the rule says so) closing above the zone bottom - a close under the bottom first ends the zone. Stop reference = the lower of the zone bottom and that candle's low.
- Indicators: RSI(14) Wilder; MACD 12 / 26 / 9; slow Stochastic 14 / 3 / 3; CCI(20); MFI(14); OBV; Chaikin A/D line; Wilder +DI / -DI / ADX(14); ROC(n) in %; TSI 25 / 13 with a 7 signal; Aroon(25); Parabolic SAR 0.02 / 0.02 / 0.2; Bollinger Bands 20, 2 (population standard deviation), %B = (close - lower) / (upper - lower); z-score = (close - SMA(n)) / standard deviation(n); Ichimoku 9 / 26 / 52 (the cloud at a candle = spans A / B computed 26 candles before). EMAs are seeded with the mean of their first n values. "Crosses above" = above on this candle, at or under on the one before.
- Divergences (I08 I12 I14 V11, hidden I02): the two latest swing lows of price with divPivot (5) candles each side, divPivot to divMaxGap (60) candles apart; signal on the candle the 2nd one becomes known; stop reference = its low.
- Benchmark (I23 I24 Q13) = NIFTY 50 daily closes on the laptop; the ratio = the stock's close / the index close of the same day (4-hour candles: of the session before - the index has daily candles only).
- "Stop = the last N lows" = the lowest low of the N candles up to and including the signal candle.
- Chart patterns (phase 4): drawn through swings (patSwing candles each side). A pattern is complete when its last swing is known; the signal is the BREAKOUT after it - the first close above the pattern's level after its last swing, on or after the candle that swing becomes known, within 60 candles (a close above the level before that - the pattern was not visible yet - or a close under the pattern's floor first = no signal). One signal per pattern.
- Pattern sizes are measured in ATR(14) of the pattern's last swing ("equal" lows / highs = within 1 x ATR, 0.5 x ATR for flat tops and rectangles), so one rule fits daily, weekly and 4-hour candles; time-based patterns give their length per timeframe.

### C01 Double Bottom (version 1)

Timeframes: D / W / 4H - parameters: `patSwing` 5

1. Two consecutive known swing lows, 2 x patSwing to 120 candles apart, within 1 x ATR of each other, nothing lower between them.
2. Neckline = the highest high between them, at least 2 x ATR above the higher low; a fall into the pattern (a high of the 60 candles before the 1st low at or above the neckline).
3. Signal: the breakout above the neckline (a close under the lower low first = failed).
4. Stop reference = the lower of the two lows; trigger = the neckline; the depth % kept.

_In words:_ Price fell to the same low twice, bounced, then closed above the high between the two lows - a double bottom.

A reversal pattern: the Lab's "Before the pattern" setting can keep it only after a fall.
